New York City: Crash Concentration Screening

20,967 recorded bicyclist crashes, 2021–2026 · top 20 of 5,408 ~250m grid cells · generated 2026-07-06

Read this first. This is the first step of a High-Injury-Network-style analysis: where documented crashes concentrate. It is not a prediction and not exposure-adjusted — busy corridors rank high partly because more people ride there; a high-count cell can be the safest place per rider in the city. Persistent means concentrated in both halves of the window (≥3 crashes in each) — those locations most likely reflect roadway conditions rather than chance, and are where systemic-safety practice says to look first. Cell boundaries are arbitrary ~250m squares. This screening supports engineering judgment; it never replaces it.
