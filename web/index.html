Micromobility Injuries

National ED-visit estimates · CPSC NEISS · no location data

Read this first. These are weighted national estimates from a sample of ~100 hospital EDs, not raw counts and not from this map's data. NEISS has no location field — these numbers cannot be placed on a map or compared city-to-city. E-bikes only got their own product code in 2024; before that they are mixed into "Bicycles" and cannot be pulled out. Small slices (e.g. fatalities) carry wide sampling uncertainty — FARS is the better source for fatal counts.

Recent Reports

Auto-detected from news coverage · unverified beyond automated checks

Read this first. These come from an automated news scan (GDELT), not a federal dataset. Each report is tagged corroborated (2+ independent outlets agree) or single-source (one outlet only) — neither means confirmed by this project. Early news can be wrong about cause or severity. Location, age, device, and outcome are extracted automatically by keyword/pattern matching, not read by a person. We show city/county + age band only — never a name or exact age, even when the source article includes one. Click through to the source(s) to verify before treating any single report as fact.

Risk & Prevention

Interpretable patterns, not a black-box prediction · every number shows its sample size

Read this first. Nothing here is machine-learning prediction — it's interpretable cross-tabs and a rate adjustment, fully auditable. The state ranking divides FARS fatalities by ACS commute-cycling data — a real but limited proxy that misses recreational, delivery, and minors' riding (the population most central to e-bikes). Cluster flags are raw fatality counts in an area, not exposure-adjusted risk — busier places show up partly because more people ride there. Prevention levers are citations from FHWA/NACTO/NHTSA guidance, not claims our data proves they work.
